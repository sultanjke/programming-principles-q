# Problem 2: Sum of Even and Odd Numbers

You are given one integer n.\
Find the sum of even numbers and the sum of odd numbers from 1 to n
(inclusive).

## Input format

One integer n.

## Output format

Print two numbers:
1) sum of even numbers\
2) sum of odd numbers

## Example

### Input

5

### Output

6 9
