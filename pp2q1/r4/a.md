# Problem D: Calculate Age

You are given two integers: a person's birth year and the current year.
Compute the person's age.

## Input format

Two integers:\
- birth_year --- the year of birth\
- current_year --- the current year

It is guaranteed that:\
1900 ≤ birth_year ≤ current_year ≤ 2100

## Output format

Print one integer --- the person's age.

## Example

### Input

2005 2025

### Output

20

## Explanation

Age is calculated as:

age = current_year − birth_year

The result must be a non-negative integer.
